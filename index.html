LEARN THE TABLE

The art of the bluff.

  1. Start with five dice. Each player rolls privately. A bid counts dice across the whole table, not just your hand.
  2. Ones are always wild. Bid on faces 2 through 6. A bid of “four fives” means at least four dice show a five or a one.
  3. Raise or challenge. Raise the quantity using any face, or keep the quantity and raise the face. You cannot bid on ones or bid more dice than are in play.
  4. Call “Liar!” The table reveals its dice. If the bid falls short, the bidder loses one die. If it is met, the challenger loses one.
  5. Stay in the game. The loser starts the next round, or the next player clockwise if the loser is out. A random player starts the first round. The last player with dice wins.
No exact calls or special one-die rounds. Ones stay wild throughout.
